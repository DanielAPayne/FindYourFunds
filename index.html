Questions people ask

Does this cost anything?

This tool is free and has no ads. Official state unclaimed-property searches and claims are generally free. Other processes can cost money: some court funds require a filing, estates may need legal help, and some recovered money is taxable. Each source's badge shows whether searching it is free.

What happens to my information?

There's no account and no server. Your tags and tracking are stored in this browser's local storage and are not sent anywhere by this tool. Sites you open from here have their own privacy policies. Browser data can be cleared, so use Back up to keep a copy.

Does this search for me?

Not yet. Today you open each official site and search it yourself, then record the result here. Automatic searching of authorized sources is on the roadmap.

Will I find money?

There's no guarantee. MissingMoney.com reports that about 1 in 7 people have unclaimed property, often a modest amount. With a common name, most results belong to other people.

A result has my name. Is it mine?

Not necessarily. Check the address and other details against places you've actually lived. You can claim only property that is yours, or that you're entitled to as the heir of a relative who has died. The state verifies identity and address history before paying.

A site asks me to verify I'm human, or the search fails.

Official sites use anti-bot checks; complete them when they appear. If a search can't be finished, mark it "Search couldn't be completed" rather than "no match", because a failed search doesn't show that nothing is there.
