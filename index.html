Questions people ask

Is this really free?

Yes. Every site linked here is an official government or institutional database, and searching and claiming are always free. This tool is free too, and shows no ads.

Is it a scam? How do I know it's safe?

This tool never asks for your Social Security number, bank details, or payment, and it never sees what you type — your profile and tracking live only in your own browser. The real scams are "finder" or "recovery" companies that take a cut to claim money you can claim yourself for free. Ignore them.

Will I actually find money?

Maybe. About 1 in 7 people have unclaimed property, usually a small-to-moderate amount. For many people the bigger money is in benefits and tax credits they qualify for but never claimed. There's no guarantee — but checking costs nothing but time.

Can I claim money under someone else's name?

Only if it's genuinely yours, or you're the rightful heir of a relative who has passed away. A matching name alone isn't enough — the state verifies your identity and address history. Claiming a stranger's money because the name matches is fraud.

Why do some sites ask me to "verify you are human"?

Official databases use anti-bot checks. Just complete the checkbox when it appears — it's normal and safe.
